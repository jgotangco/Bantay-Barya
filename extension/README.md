# Bantay Barya Browser Extension (Manifest V3)

A lightweight browser extension for **Bantay Barya** that allows you to quickly record transactions, view multi-wallet balances, and take instant snapshots directly from your browser toolbar.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **⚡ Quick Expense Logging**: Record debits or credits with category autocomplete in 2 seconds while shopping or paying bills online.
- **👛 Multi-Wallet Selection**: Instantly choose which wallet to debit or credit (e.g. *Personal Spending*, *Credit Card*, *Cash*).
- **💾 Save Slot Aware**: Automatically operates on your currently active Save Slot.
- **📸 1-Click Instant Snapshot**: Take a timestamped backup of your financial state with one click.
- **🌐 Seamless Sync**: Shared storage with the full Bantay Barya web application.
- **🔗 Quick Dashboard Launch**: 1-click button to open your complete continuous ledger in a full tab.

---

## 🛠️ How to Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi)

1. Open your browser's extensions page:
   - **Google Chrome / Brave**: Navigate to `chrome://extensions`
   - **Microsoft Edge**: Navigate to `edge://extensions`
2. Toggle on **Developer mode** (top right corner).
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button.
4. Select this `extension` folder:
   `C:\Users\jgota\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\expense-tracker\extension`
5. Click the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar and pin **Bantay Barya**!

---

## ⌨️ Shortcut Keys

- Press `Alt+Shift+B` (Windows) or `Option+Shift+B` (Mac) to open the quick logger popup at any time.
